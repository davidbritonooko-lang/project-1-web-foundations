About Quick Notes

Quick Notes gives you a calm, uncomplicated place to collect ideas before they slip away. Add a thought from the home page and it is saved in this browser, ready for you to revisit the next time you open the app on this device.

Made for quick thinking

  • Capture quickly: Add a note with just a few words and keep your momentum.
  • Keep your list close: Notes are stored locally in your browser, not on a Quick Notes account.
  • Stay in control: Review or remove notes whenever you like from the home page.

Notes are stored on the device and browser where you create them. They are not automatically synced to other devices, so avoid entering confidential information.

Use Quick Notes as an app

Install Quick Notes on your device for a home-screen shortcut and a focused, app-like experience. The website remains available in your browser too.

Android: use the install button or your browser's Install app option. iPhone and iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Quick Notes is a web app and is not currently listed in the Apple App Store or Google Play. App installation and offline support require serving this site over HTTPS.
